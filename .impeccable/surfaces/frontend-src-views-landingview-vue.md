# Surface brief — public landing (frontend/src/views/LandingView.vue)

Scope: public route `/` of the Vue app; signed-in users are redirected to `/dashboard`. Visitor mode: Persuade. World inherited from the app shell brief (ink, one alert yellow, boarding-pass grammar, Red Hat Text/Mono, Barlow Condensed). EN/ES via vue-i18n.

Audience/job: teams (and portfolio reviewers) deciding whether DocuMind is worth trying. Action: create a workspace (email field that carries into /register); secondary: sign in. Proof: the product's own interface, rendered live with its real components and labelled sample data. No customers, quotes, logos or usage numbers exist; none are invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing is one boarding pass: the promise is printed on the pass a cited answer would carry. It refuses the split hero with a product screenshot and the three icon cards below it.

OWN-WORLD: Ink ground, a white pass with labelled segments (DOCUMENTO · PÁGINA · CLÁUSULA · SIMILITUD) and a perforated stub with a giant S1; yellow only on the primary action state and the active citation. Barlow Condensed uppercase headlines, Red Hat Text body, Red Hat Mono for segment labels and figures.

STORY: Visitor reads the promise (ask your documents, verify every answer), sees how a document travels from uploaded to citable, plays with a real answer whose S1/S2/S3 open their passes, and creates a workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink top bar (mark, How it works, Code, language, Sign in). A full-width pass: top segment row with a real citation, headline at display size inside the pass, one supporting sentence, bottom segment with email field + Create workspace + Sign in; stub on the right with S1 and a short note. On phones the stub folds to the bottom of the pass.

FORM: dealt structure "l-pass" (one boarding pass), surface seed:5dad081ca2b23222. Signature interaction: the live answer below the fold; pressing S1/S2/S3 lights that chip and its pass in yellow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
